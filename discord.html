Discord Bookmarks

Helping Discord users stay organized and productive by designing a bookmarks feature that saves important messages from getting lost in busy conversations.

Role
Web Developer, UX Designer
Timeline
Fall 2025
Course
Intro to Digital Product Design
Tools & Skills
Figma, User Research, Prototyping

Context

Important messages vanish in busy Discord servers.

Like many college students, I'm part of several Discord activity groups like my dance team, project groups, and club organizations. We use Discord to coordinate practices and meetings, but important information like deadlines, event schedules, and form links gets buried within hours.

Two weeks ago, I missed a form because I couldn't find the message. After scrolling endlessly, I gave up and waited for someone else to remind me, even though the information was technically “there” the whole time.

When users want to find old information, they...

1) Scroll through channels

Users scroll up through channels, hoping to recognize the message as they go.

2) Try Discord search

Search often requires specific keywords or dates they don't remember.

3) Rely on other workarounds or give up.

They screenshot messages, ask others to resend information, or give up and wait for reminders.

User interviews

Why finding information feels exhausting

After interviewing 3 users (ages 18–24) who actively use Discord for activity groups (dance teams, clubs, and coordination), I discovered a pattern:

User 1

"You have to know exactly what you want and when."

User 2

"Sometimes I can't find what I'm looking for. So I just give up."

User 3

"I'd rather not look at my Discord than have to deal with scrolling."

Insight 1

Users don’t trust search

Users scroll first and only switch to search when messages are very old.

Insight 2

Effort → giving up

When finding information takes too long, users give up, even for important details.

Insight 3

No system to stay organized

People rely on screenshots, calendars, or moderators instead of Discord itself.

Insight 4

High-stakes content

Missing messages means missed forms, deadlines, and showing up unprepared.

Journey

Mapping the struggle to find old messages

I mapped the journey from the moment users realize they need information to when they either find it or give up. The pattern was consistent across all users:

Step 1

Users realize they now need a form, schedule, or practice video from weeks ago

Step 2

They try to recall the context like channel, sender, and rough time.

Step 3

They scan and scroll through long message histories for anything that looks familiar.

Step 4

When scrolling fails, they try Discord's search but feel overwhelmed by results and filters.

Step 5

Eventually, they will find it after a lot of effort, or give up.

By step 5, users feel extremely frustrated and defeated; not just annoyed, but anxious about missing deadlines. The emotional toll of repeatedly failing to find critical information was clear.
