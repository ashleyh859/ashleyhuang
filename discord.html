Discord Bookmarks

Helping Discord users stay organized and productive by designing a bookmarks feature that saves important messages from getting lost in busy conversations.

Role
Web Developer, UX Designer
Timeline
Fall 2025
Course
Intro to Digital Product Design
Tools & Skills
Figma, User Research, Prototyping

Context

Important messages vanish in busy Discord servers.

Like many college students, I'm part of several Discord activity groups like my dance team, project groups, and club organizations. We use Discord to coordinate practices and meetings, but important information like deadlines, event schedules, and form links gets buried within hours.

Two weeks ago, I missed a form because I couldn't find the message. After scrolling endlessly, I gave up and waited for someone else to remind me, even though the information was technically “there” the whole time.

When users want to find old information, they...

1) Scroll through channels

Users scroll up through channels, hoping to recognize the message as they go.

2) Try Discord search

Search often requires specific keywords or dates they don't remember.

3) Rely on other workarounds or give up.

They screenshot messages, ask others to resend information, or give up and wait for reminders.
